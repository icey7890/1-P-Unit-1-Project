Sxahan, and Other Similar Personas

Primary: Sxahan

Sxahan is my primary persona, but despite that, they don't have any story to go with their design. The only thing that matters is how iconic they are. However, I do plan on giving them a story. I just don't know if I should stick to my real life story or not. I DO have a headcannon that they are just me in real life, except has powers that allow them to explore different universes and shapeshift.
Some creatures they have shapeshifted to include...

  • Half-fox
  • Half-cat
  • Half-dog
  • Merman
  • Teal hair

Sxahan, Design

It took me a long time to find a design I was comfortable with. In the end I realized I was overthinking it and just thought of my aura. I absolutely love poofy, expressive hair, so what I did was give Sxahan huge, spiky, neon pink hair.

Sxahan and Myself in Their Aura

Honesly, Sxahan helped me with my identity because the persona is just so unapologetically themself. In appreciation, I want to write them a poem.

I know you cease to exist.
I know you're only in my imagination.
But that makes you mine, purely my creation.
You are me, I am you.
Yet you are all that I want to be.
It is time I learn from myself and be me.
